Import from Spotify

Paste a Spotify track, album or playlist link. spotDL downloads the songs with their details and cover art. CroDJ then finds the BPM and key, and they show up under Spotify imports.

How to use CroDJ

Play a song

Each library row has two ▶ buttons: the first plays on deck A, the second on deck B. You can also drag songs from Explorer onto a deck.

SYNC · MATCH · ASSIST

  • SYNC: makes this deck follow the other deck's tempo and lines up the beats. Use it when both decks are playing and the kicks sound like they're galloping. When the decks are locked, the SYNC button turns white.
  • MATCH: changes only the tempo to match the other deck and leaves the beat position alone. Useful before you start a deck. A ! on MATCH means the two songs' keys clash.
  • ASSIST (on by default): when you press ▶ on a stopped deck while the other is playing, it syncs automatically and waits for the next bar, so the new song starts perfectly on the beat.

VOCAL · INSTRU · BEAT

Each knob starts in the centre, which is normal. Turn it left to remove that part (for example, VOCAL left removes the singing). Turn it right to isolate it (the other parts fade out). Drag up or right to turn it right, down or left to turn it left. Double-click a knob to reset it.

AI MIX

Load a song on each deck and press AI MIX. CroDJ analyses both songs, waits for the next phrase, lines up the beats and blends them: it swaps the bass, removes the outgoing vocals so two singers never clash, sweeps a filter, then lands the new song's drop. While the AI is working, the decks are locked. Click the AI badge twice to take control back.

Changing the song on a deck that's playing

Just click ▶ on another song. CroDJ fades to the other deck, swaps the song, then fades back.
